01 / Overview

Spatial reasoning,
built for the underwater world.

Underwater spatial reasoning depends on boundaries and geometric cues that are often obscured by scattering, color distortion, and low visibility.

USI-Brain unifies fragmented underwater resources through a spatial data engine that connects visual appearance with object-level geometry. The engine combines image enhancement, 2D and geometry-assisted question generation, capability-based organization, and expert filtering to produce a large-scale spatial supervision corpus.

We train USI-Brain from Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct and introduce the Underwater Prior-guided Rectifier (UPR), which applies gated corrections to visual features while preserving their original representation.

02 / Method

From scattered resources
to structured intelligence.

The method couples supervision construction with visual-feature adaptation: HUE prepares visual evidence, geometry-assisted QA exposes 3D relationships, and UPR corrects visual tokens inside the model.

HUE

Restore the evidence

Hybrid Underwater Enhancement balances learned color correction and local detail restoration with a pixel-wise fusion map.

GeoQA

Lift pixels into geometry

Depth samples and reconstructed object boxes support questions about ordering, occlusion, distance, viewpoint, and stratification.

Review

Keep supervision grounded

Expert review checks visual evidence, question-answer consistency, localization annotations, and redundancy before training.

03 / Benchmark

One benchmark.
Five connected abilities.

USI-Bench contains 6,525 held-out QA pairs spanning object grounding, image-plane relations, 3D structure, degradation-aware perception, and ecological understanding.

Fifteen independent annotators audited every benchmark item. Uncertain and incorrect entries identified in the audit were revised before evaluation.

04 / Results

Strong spatial gains,
inside and outside the benchmark.
